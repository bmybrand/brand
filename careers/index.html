How we hire

Every client project at Revonix gets its own dedicated team: the developers, designers and specialists the work needs, led by an engineering lead and looked after by one person the client talks to from day one. So we don't hire to fill a quota. Roles open when a new project starts and needs someone with your skills.

That means the open roles change month to month. If there is nothing that fits you today, send us your details anyway. When a project comes in that needs what you do, we look at the people who have already written to us first.

Remote-first

We are a US-registered company with a delivery team in Islamabad, Pakistan, and clients around the world. Most work is remote. Our clients choose their hours, and we work theirs, up to 24/7, so many roles include working in a client's time zone.

The kind of work

Roles come from the work our clients need, across:

  • AI and automation: AI agents, LLM integrations and automated workflows (remote AI automation jobs).
  • Workflow automation: connecting business tools with n8n, Make and Zapier.
  • Low-code development: Bubble.io apps and internal tools.
  • Custom web and mobile apps: front ends, back ends and mobile builds.
  • QA, DevOps and support: testing, deployment and keeping systems running.

What we look for

  • You can show your work. A portfolio, live products, code or case studies tell us more than a list of tools.
  • Clear, honest communication. Our clients talk to us on Slack or Teams, WhatsApp, email and calls. You explain what you're doing in plain words, and you say early when something is off track.
  • Ownership. You care whether what you build actually works for the client's business, not just whether the ticket is closed.
  • Reliability across time zones. You keep to the hours and updates you agree with your team.
  • Good written English, because most of our clients and documents are in English.

How to apply

Email careers@revonix.co with the subject Careers. Include:

  • the kind of work you do and the role you'd like;
  • links to your portfolio, profiles or code;
  • where you are based and which hours you can work.

We read every application. When a project needs your skills, we'll get in touch to talk.

Send us your work

Email careers@revonix.co with the subject Careers, your work and the hours you can keep. When a project needs your skills, we get in touch.

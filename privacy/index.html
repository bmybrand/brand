This policy explains how Revonix Innovation LLC ("Revonix", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit revonix.co, contact us, book a call, or work with us as a client or partner.

Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Revonix Innovation LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, USA. Our registered office is 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. Our delivery team works from Riverwalk Plaza, River Garden Housing Society, Islamabad, Zone 5, Pakistan.

For anything about your data, email hello@revonix.co or call +1 (832) 323-7219.

What we collect

When you fill in a form

  • Project form (Get started): your name, email, company, website, budget range, your message and any answers you choose (such as platform and timeline).
  • Booking a call: your name, email, phone (if you give it), company, website, budget, notes and the time you pick.

With each form we also record your time zone, an approximate location (country, region and city) worked out from your connection, and how you reached us: the page you landed on, the referring site, and campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source or gclid). We use this to reply at a sensible time and to understand which channels bring us enquiries.

When you browse the website

  • Analytics: we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. They set cookies (such as _ga) and receive your IP address, browser and device details and the pages you visit.
  • Advertising measurement: on our campaign pages (such as /free-ios-demo) we use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API to learn which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring enquiries. They set cookies (_fbp, and _fbc when you arrive from a Meta ad) and receive your IP address, browser details, the pages and steps you visit and a random visitor ID.
  • When you send a form or book a call on a campaign page, we also send Meta a hashed (SHA-256) form of your email, phone number, name and approximate location, so it can match the enquiry to an ad you saw. Hashing turns these details into codes before they leave our servers; Meta uses them to match and measure our ads under its business terms.
  • Your choice: use Cookie settings in the footer to turn analytics and advertising off for your browser. We also honour the Global Privacy Control signal: if your browser sends it, we do not use the Meta Pixel or Conversions API and keep Google's advertising features off.
  • Your browser also stores a few settings in local storage that never leave your device on their own: your theme choice (rvx-theme), your cookie choice (revonix_consent), a random visitor ID (rvx_vid) and the campaign tags of your visit.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) to run and protect the service.

When you open a link we send you

  • Emails: our emails may contain a small image and tracked links that tell us whether a message was opened or a link clicked. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Audit reports and proposals (links under /audit, /r and /sign): when you open one, we record the time, your browser and device type, approximate location, IP address, the referring page and how far you read. This tells us the report reached you and which parts were useful.
  • Signing a contract online: we record the name, email and title you type, your signature (drawn or typed), the consent you give, the time, your IP address, browser and approximate location. This is the evidence that the contract was signed.

Landing pages and campaigns

Our campaign landing pages (on this website, such as /free-ios-demo, and some outside it) record the steps a visitor takes through a form, with a random visitor and session ID, browser and device type, approximate location, a salted hash of the IP address and, on some pages, the IP address and a device fingerprint. If you reach us through Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead forms or a cold email campaign, we receive the details you or that service give us.

When you are a client

We keep the contact details, messages, files, contracts and invoices needed to run your project.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To reply to you and prepare a proposal you asked for: steps before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • To deliver the work in a signed contract: performance of the contract.
  • To check your website when you send it to us, so our reply and any free audit are useful: our legitimate interest, and your request.
  • To understand which channels and pages bring enquiries, and to keep the site and our forms secure (spam and abuse checks): our legitimate interest.
  • To send you follow-up emails about your enquiry or our services: our legitimate interest or, where the law requires it, your consent. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep records for tax, accounting and legal claims: legal obligation and legitimate interest.

We do not sell your personal data. We do share limited identifiers and browsing activity with Meta and Google to measure our advertising, which some US state laws (such as California's) treat as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out at any time with Cookie settings or the Global Privacy Control signal, or by emailing us.

Automated tools and AI

We may use AI models to help sort incoming emails, draft emails and audit summaries, and fill in records in our CRM. When we do, the relevant text (for example your message or the details in your enquiry) is sent to an AI provider listed below. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means alone, and we can switch these tools off at any time.

Who processes it for us

We share personal data only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, and only as needed:

  • Hosting and file storage: Vercel (website hosting, server logs, approximate location from your connection, and file storage through Vercel Blob).
  • Database: a managed PostgreSQL provider that stores our CRM and website content.
  • Background jobs: Trigger.dev, which runs scheduled tasks such as sending emails and generating reports.
  • Email: our email and mailbox providers (SMTP and IMAP), and Smartlead for outbound email campaigns.
  • Booking: Google Workspace (our calendar, calendar invitations and Google Meet links).
  • AI processing: OpenRouter, OpenAI or Anthropic, when the AI tools above are switched on.
  • Analytics: Google (Tag Manager and Google Analytics).
  • Advertising measurement: Meta (the Meta Pixel and Conversions API on our campaign pages, as described above).
  • Lead forms on social media: Meta, if you send us your details through a Facebook or Instagram lead form.
  • Our audit tool, which analyses the public website and competitors you name.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business.

International transfers

We are a US company with a delivery team in Pakistan, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. When we transfer personal data from the EU, UK or other regions with transfer rules, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or our providers' equivalent commitments.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months after our last contact, then we delete or anonymise them.
  • Client records, contracts and invoices: for the length of the relationship and up to 7 years afterwards, for tax, accounting and legal reasons.
  • Report, proposal and email engagement data: up to 24 months.
  • Unsubscribe requests: kept for as long as needed so we don't email you again.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our admin tools, encrypted storage of mailbox passwords, private storage for files and contracts, and salted hashing where we only need to count visitors. No method is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we tell affected people and authorities about a breach when the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as California's), you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use it, including for direct marketing;
  • ask for your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Notice for California residents

In the last 12 months we collected identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address), commercial information (budgets, proposals, contracts), internet activity (pages and links opened), approximate location and professional information (company and role), from you and the services above, for the purposes described in this policy. We do not sell personal information. We share identifiers (such as cookie IDs, IP address and hashed contact details) and internet activity with Meta and Google to measure our advertising, which the CCPA treats as "sharing"; you can opt out with Cookie settings in our footer, by turning on Global Privacy Control in your browser, or by emailing us. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You have the right to know, delete and correct your information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

To use any right, email hello@revonix.co. We will reply within one business day to confirm we received it, may need to verify your identity, and will complete it within the time the law allows (usually 30 or 45 days).

Children

Our website and services are for businesses. They are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Our site links to other sites, such as Upwork, Fiverr and LinkedIn. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell clients about significant changes by email.

Six steps, from first call to the next step

  1. 1Free

    A free consultation

    A call to understand your business and what you need. If it helps, we also run a free audit of your current systems and workflows. No cost and no commitment.

    Book a free consultation
  2. 2Proposal

    A written proposal with a clear price

    You get the scope, the timeline and the price in writing before anything starts. We work at a fixed price, on a monthly retainer or as a dedicated team, whichever fits the work.

    Tell us your project
  3. 3Build

    Your dedicated team builds it

    Once the contract is signed, a team built around your project gets to work. They talk to you on your channels and update you as often as you want.

    See case studies
  4. 4Launch

    We launch it with you

    We test everything, put it live and walk you through it, so you know how it works and what to do next.

  5. 5Support

    One month of free support

    For the first month after launch we look after it at no cost: fixes, small changes and your questions.

  6. 6Next step

    The next step, with the next team

    When you're ready to automate more or grow, the right team joins in. Your one person stays the same.

Your channels, your hours

We work your hours, up to 24/7, and update you as often as you like.

  • Slack or Teams
  • WhatsApp
  • Email and calls
  • A shared project board
  • A daily call with the developers, if you want one

One team, one face

Every client gets one dedicated person from day one. Behind them is a dedicated team built around your project, which grows as your project grows.

  1. Strategy lead

    Understands your business

    Meets you, learns how your business works and where the gaps in your market are, then shapes the solution with you.

  2. Engineering lead

    Builds your team and system

    Puts together the dedicated team for your project and leads the build, so what we agreed is what gets made.

  3. Client success manager

    Looks after you every day after

    Stays your contact after kickoff. Questions, changes and updates go through one person who knows your project.

The team behind them changes as you move from build to automate to grow. Your person stays the same. We'll tell you when automation isn't worth it.

Three ways to pay for the work

Your written proposal states the price before anything starts. We suggest the model that fits the work; you choose.

  • Fixed price

    One price for an agreed scope, written in your proposal. Best when you know what you need built.

  • Monthly retainer

    A set amount each month for ongoing work: maintenance, new automations, or a steady flow of improvements.

  • Dedicated team

    People who work as part of your team, for as long as you need, without you hiring them yourself.

What you keep at the end

At handover you get everything that runs your project. Nothing is held back, so you are never locked in to us.

  • The code
  • The accounts
  • The documentation

How long does a project take?+
It depends on the scope: automating one inbox and a multi-system AI build are very different jobs. Your written proposal states the timeline before anything starts, and the shared project board shows where things stand.
How much does it cost?+
That also depends on the scope. The consultation is free, and your proposal gives a clear price in writing, at a fixed price, on a monthly retainer or as a dedicated team, before you commit to anything.
Who owns the code?+
You do. At handover you get the code, the accounts and the documentation, so you can run it yourself or take it to anyone else.
What if I need changes after launch?+
Fixes and small changes in the first month after launch are free. After that, we agree any new work with you in writing first, as a fixed price or on a monthly retainer.
We're in a different time zone. How does that work?+
We work your hours, up to 24/7. You talk to us on Slack or Teams, WhatsApp, email and scheduled calls, and we update you as often as you choose.
Who will I talk to?+
One dedicated person from day one, who knows your project and stays with you. If you want, you can also have a daily call with the developers.

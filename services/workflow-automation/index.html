The short answer

Workflow automation connects the software a business already uses so that one event triggers the next steps automatically, such as a form submission creating a CRM contact, an onboarding task and a welcome email. Revonix designs and builds these workflows in n8n, Make and Zapier, choosing the platform that suits your volume, budget and data requirements.

Key takeaways

  • We choose between n8n, Make and Zapier based on your volume, complexity and hosting needs.
  • Every workflow includes error handling and alerts, not only the happy path.
  • Each workflow is documented, so your team can see what runs where and why.
  • AI steps can be added later where a decision needs reading or judgment.

What workflow automation is

Workflow automation is the practice of connecting your business tools so that one event sets off the steps that should follow it. A deal closes in HubSpot, and a project is created, an invoice is drafted and the delivery team is notified. A form is submitted, and the details land in the right spreadsheet with a task for the right person. Nobody has to remember to do it, and nobody retypes anything.

It matters because the gaps between tools are where work gets lost. Each manual handoff is a chance for a delay, a typo or a step that simply gets skipped on a busy day. Our workflow automation services close those gaps using n8n, Make and Zapier, wired into the systems your team already runs.

Signs you need workflow automation

  • The same details are typed into three different systems.
  • New clients or orders wait because someone has to start the process by hand.
  • A spreadsheet acts as the glue between tools and is always slightly out of date.
  • You have a few automations already, but nobody knows exactly what they do.
  • Mistakes happen most when the team is busiest.

Example: lead-to-onboarding for a B2B training company

An illustrative example of a typical project, not a client case study.

A company that runs corporate training workshops sells through its website and a small sales team. When a client books, someone creates the deal in HubSpot, sends the agreement, raises the invoice, adds the workshop to a planning spreadsheet and emails the trainer. Each step depends on someone remembering the last one, and details sometimes differ between systems.

We would connect those steps into one flow so that a booking starts the whole sequence and the team only steps in where judgment is needed.

What we would build

  • Website booking forms that create or update the HubSpot contact and deal.
  • An agreement sent automatically when the deal reaches the right stage.
  • An invoice drafted on signature, using the deal's line items.
  • A new row in the workshop planning spreadsheet and a notification to the assigned trainer.
  • An alert to the account owner if any step has not completed within a set time.

Should we use n8n, Make or Zapier?+
Zapier is the quickest to set up and has the widest range of app connections, which suits simpler workflows. Make handles branching logic and data transformation well at a lower cost per operation. n8n can be self-hosted, which helps with high volumes or strict data rules. We recommend one after seeing your tools and workload.
What happens when a workflow fails?+
We build for it from the start. Failed runs retry where that is safe, errors are logged with the data that caused them, and a named person gets an alert. For important workflows we add checks that confirm the expected result actually happened.
Can you fix or take over automations someone else built?+
Yes. Many businesses have a collection of older Zaps or scenarios that nobody fully understands. We audit what exists, document it, fix what is broken and consolidate duplicates before adding anything new.
Will workflow automation work with our CRM?+
Almost certainly. HubSpot and most other common CRMs connect to n8n, Make and Zapier directly. Where a tool has no ready-made connector, we can usually connect through its API or a webhook.
Do we own the workflows you build?+
Yes. The workflows run in accounts that belong to your business, and we hand over documentation for each one. You can keep us on for support or run them in-house.

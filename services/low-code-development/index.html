The short answer

Low-code development builds applications on platforms such as Bubble.io, where the interface, database and logic are configured visually instead of written line by line. Revonix uses Bubble.io to take products and internal tools from idea to a working app quickly, with a clean data model, sensible privacy rules and documented workflows, so you can test the idea with real users without building up technical debt. For operational apps that live inside a business, we also build in Quickbase.

Key takeaways

  • Bubble.io gets a working web app in front of real users sooner than a full custom build.
  • Rapid prototypes let you validate an idea before you spend on a larger build.
  • A clean data model, privacy rules and reusable workflows keep a low-code app maintainable.
  • When an app outgrows the platform, we help plan the move to custom code.

What low-code development is

Low-code development means building software on a platform that handles much of the groundwork for you. Pages are laid out visually, data is stored in a built-in database, and logic is set up as workflows instead of being written line by line. Bubble.io is one of the most capable platforms for this kind of work, especially for web apps that real users log in to.

The main benefit is speed. You get a working app in weeks, not months, so you can put an idea in front of users and learn from it before committing to a larger build. The main risk is that a fast build turns into a fragile one. Our job is to get the speed without the mess: structured data, clear privacy rules, reusable workflows and an app you can still change a year from now.

Who it's for

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We work out the one flow your app has to get right first, who uses it and what they need to see, and strip everything else out of the first version.
  2. 2

    Strategy

    We design the data model and privacy rules, and list what is in and out of scope. The plan, timeline and price are agreed before building.
  3. 3

    Delivery

    We build the core flow first and get it into users' hands early, then add features, integrations and reports in short cycles based on how they actually use it.
  4. 4

    Support & Growth

    We fix issues, tune performance as usage grows, add features and, if the app outgrows the platform, help you plan a move to a custom build.

Example: a client portal MVP for a consulting firm

An illustrative example of a typical project, not a client case study.

A small consulting firm runs client onboarding by email. Clients send documents as attachments, status updates live in one person's inbox, and nobody can see at a glance which engagements are waiting on what.

We would build a focused Bubble.io portal that becomes the one place each engagement lives, small enough to launch quickly and structured so it can grow.

What we would build

  • A client login with each engagement's status, next steps and shared documents.
  • An upload step that files documents against the right engagement.
  • A team view of open engagements and what each one is waiting on.
  • An automation that notifies the right consultant when a client uploads or replies.

Is Bubble.io good enough for a production app?+
For most MVPs, customer portals, marketplaces and internal tools, yes. Bubble runs real applications for real businesses every day. The limits show up with very high traffic, heavy data processing or unusual performance needs, and we flag those early so you do not discover them after launch.
What can you build in Bubble?+
Web apps that need user accounts, a database and business logic, such as MVPs for a new product, client and partner portals, booking and request flows, dashboards and internal admin tools. AI features, such as a step that calls OpenAI or Claude, can be added through Bubble's API connector.
What does technical debt mean in a low-code app?+
It is the mess that makes an app slower and riskier to change over time. In low-code, it usually comes from a poorly planned database, logic copied across many pages and workflows, and automations nobody documented. We avoid it by designing the data structure first, reusing logic and writing down how the app works.
Do you also build in Quickbase?+
Yes. Bubble.io is our main platform for customer-facing web apps and products that need to launch fast. For operational apps that run inside a business, such as project tracking or approvals, we also build in Quickbase.
Can you take over a Bubble app someone else built?+
Yes. We start with an audit of the data types, privacy rules, workflows and plugins, then give you a plain list of what is working, what is risky and what we would fix first.

Muhammad Aazain

Muhammad Aazain is the co-founder and CEO of Revonix, an AI automation agency for growing teams. He brings several years of experience across technology and business, and he started Revonix on a simple conviction: companies shouldn't need a dozen vendors to put AI to work — they need one partner who owns the outcome. Aazain sets the company's direction, works with clients from the first conversation, and keeps the team honest about what AI automation can and can't do. Whether the client is a small business or an enterprise, his focus is the same — ship AI systems that quietly do the work and pay for themselves.
